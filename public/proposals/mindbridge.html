The Brief

What we're
building together

You're building a platform that connects people seeking mental health support with the right therapists — through an intelligent matching system that protects user privacy until they're ready to commit.

The platform needs three distinct portals: one for clients finding and booking therapists, one for therapists managing their availability and session notes, and one for admins overseeing the entire ecosystem.

We're approaching this as a full custom-code build — but smart about it. Where the problem is already solved (scheduling, video, payments), we integrate best-in-class open-source tools. Where it differentiates your product (matching, anonymization, notes), we build it ourselves.

🎯
Privacy-first matching
Therapist identity is hidden at the recommendation stage — name and photo revealed only after the client commits to booking. Enforced at the API level, not just the UI.
🔧
Custom code where it matters
Matching algorithm, RBAC session notes, admin oversight — all built custom. Scheduling, video, and payments handled by Cal.com, Daily.co, and Stripe.
📦
One lean codebase
All three portals in a single Next.js monorepo with tRPC and Prisma — type-safe end to end, easy to maintain, one deployment pipeline.

Before We Start

A few things to
confirm at kickoff

These decisions affect architecture choices. Ideally we nail these down before writing a line of code.

Q1Is payment collected on-platform, or does the client pay the therapist directly?
Q2What jurisdiction will this operate in? HIPAA and GDPR affect data storage choices.
Q3Self-hosted Cal.com or Cal.com cloud? Self-hosted is free but needs infra; cloud has a monthly cost.
Q4What matching criteria matter most — specialization, availability, language, pricing?
Q5Can clients switch therapists after being matched? What's the cancellation policy?
Q6Does the platform take a commission on sessions? If yes, we'll need Stripe Connect for payouts.
Q7Therapists — employees of the startup, or independent contractors?
Q8Who manages therapist onboarding — admin manually, or self-serve application flow?
