00Introduction & Understanding
Paper records, made searchable.

Crestify is a full-stack product & AI development studio in Kanpur. We build custom software, data pipelines and AI systems end to end — including on-premise, privacy-first deployments where sensitive data must stay within the client’s own infrastructure.

This proposal covers the complete, end-to-end digitisation of approximately 4,150 physical surgical registers — from secure collection through scanning, OCR, indexing, quality control and secure delivery. A defining principle of our approach: all AI and OCR processing runs locally, on DHS-provided private servers, so no patient record is ever sent to a third party or the public cloud.

01Our Approach
Working system first, then tuned.

We deliver in two phases so a usable system is in your hands early, then hardened to the full accuracy and security bar.

1

Phase 1 — Base system (≈ first 30–40 days)

The full digitisation pipeline stood up and running — collection & tracking, scanning, OCR, indexing, search, QC and admin — at a working baseline accuracy, on your on-premise servers.

2

Phase 2 — Optimisation & hardening (≈ 20–25 days)

OCR models tuned to meet the ≥95% accuracy target, multi-level QC completed, security hardened, and final secure delivery with completion certificate and data-integrity report.

On-premise AI
The OCR and AI models run entirely on DHS-provided private server hardware — specified by Crestify, procured and hosted by DHS. Patient data stays inside your network end to end, which is what makes IT Act, 2000 compliance and role-based control straightforward rather than an afterthought.
02Detailed Scope of Work
Every stage, specified.

Eight workstreams, delivered to the technical specifications in the scope document.

01

Collection & Handling

Secure collection of registers from designated hospitals, with barcode/QR-based documentation and tracking for every register, and safe transportation that ensures no physical damage or loss.

Barcode/QR TrackingChain of CustodySafe Transport
02

Scanning

Minimum 300 DPI (600 DPI for fragile or damaged documents), colour scanning for records with colour markings, non-destructive methods, and support for all register sizes and conditions.

300–600 DPIColourNon-destructive
03

File Format & Output

Archival master files in TIFF / PDF-A, searchable OCR-enabled PDF access files, the file-naming convention approved by DHS, and a logical folder hierarchy: Date → Patient Name → Department → Doctor Name → Procedure.

TIFF / PDF-ASearchable PDFFolder Hierarchy
04

OCR & Indexing

OCR at a minimum 95% accuracy, with metadata captured for Date, Patient Name (Age, Sex), Department, Doctor Name and Procedure Done (with remarks where applicable) — and full-text search across the archive.

OCR ≥95%MetadataFull-text Search
05

Quality Control

A multi-level QC process — image-quality verification, OCR validation and indexing-accuracy checks — holding to a minimum 99% image-clarity compliance, with re-scanning of any rejected documents.

Multi-level QC≥99% ClarityRe-scan Rejects
06

Data Security & Compliance

Compliance with the IT Act, 2000 and relevant data-protection norms; restricted, role-based access; no data duplication or unauthorised sharing; and secure, encrypted storage throughout.

IT Act 2000Role-based AccessEncrypted
07

Storage & Backup

Daily incremental backups, weekly full backups, and storage redundancy via RAID / NAS / cloud backup as approved — so no digitised record is ever at risk of loss.

Daily IncrementalWeekly FullRAID / NAS
08

Final Delivery

Delivery in encrypted, password-protected digital format (hard disk or secure server upload), two backup copies (one onsite, one offsite), and submission of a completion certificate and a data-integrity report.

Encrypted DeliveryOnsite + OffsiteCompletion Cert.
