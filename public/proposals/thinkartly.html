02The payment page
The bit that makes it useful internally.

Here’s exactly how it works, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Someone from the studio logs into a private admin screen on the site.

  2. 2

    They enter the client’s name, project name, amount, an optional note — a milestone or invoice number — and an expiry date.

  3. 3

    The site creates a unique payment page and a QR code pointing to it.

  4. 4

    That link or QR goes to the client over WhatsApp, email, or printed on the invoice.

  5. 5

    The client opens it, sees Aura Design Works’ branding and what they’re paying for, and pays by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet.

  6. 6

    The studio sees the status — pending, paid, expired — in a single list, and both sides get an email confirmation.

Payments run through Razorpay, which handles Indian and international cards. Aura Design Works holds the account and the money settles directly to the studio’s bank — we never touch funds. Razorpay’s own account setup and transaction fees sit outside this quote.
