A custom-built store,
made for Raabta.

Fragrance is bought on feeling — the story, the notes, the mood. So Raabta's store is designed and developed from scratch to carry that feeling: a slow, elegant experience that lets each scent breathe. Rich imagery, evocative product pages with top/heart/base notes, and a considered flow built to convert a small, premium collection.

Behind it, payments, shipping, and delivery are fully integrated so orders move from cart to doorstep cleanly. Design, development, catalog, and integrations — delivered as one cohesive build, with a single handover.

01 Scope & Modules
What we're building.

Five focused modules, designed and developed as one store.

01

Custom UI/UX Design

A brand-aligned design system using Raabta's colours, type, and logo. Every page designed from scratch — home, collection, product, cart, checkout, about, and contact — with a fragrance-forward, editorial feel, delivered as an interactive prototype signed off before build.

Design System All Pages Prototype
02

Custom Shopify Theme Development

A pixel-perfect custom theme built from the design — not an off-the-shelf template. Fully responsive, with size variants (30ml / 50ml / 100ml), a fragrance-notes layout (top / heart / base), rich product galleries, sticky add-to-cart, and speed-optimised throughout.

Custom Theme Size Variants Notes Layout Responsive
03

Product Catalog · 5–10 Fragrances

Upload of your 5–10 signature scents with variants (sizes), pricing, evocative descriptions, fragrance notes, and images. Organised into clean collections, with SEO-ready titles and meta on every product.

5–10 SKUs Size Variants Notes & Copy SEO Fields
04

Integrations · Payments, Shipping & Delivery

Payment gateway setup (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets) and shipping & delivery integration with rate calculation, order tracking, and COD if needed. Plus WhatsApp & email order notifications and analytics (GA4, Meta Pixel).

Payments Shipping Delivery Notifications Analytics
05

Store Setup, QA & Launch

Domain connection, store settings, taxes, and policy pages (shipping, returns, privacy). On-page SEO basics, speed optimisation, and full cross-device QA — then go-live, a walkthrough, and a post-launch support window.
