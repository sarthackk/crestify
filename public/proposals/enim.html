§01WHAT WE FOUND
The product is perfect.
The store isn't.

eniM's custom-fit denim is genuinely exceptional. But the Shopify store — the place where that product should sell itself — is leaving money on the table with friction in the flow, a generic theme, and manual processes that don't scale.

§01 · THEME

Generic template, premium product

A brand charging ₹3,999–₹4,999 for custom-made denim deserves a store that feels custom-made too. The current theme doesn't reflect the craftsmanship of the product.

§02 · SIZING FLOW

The measurement journey has friction

Three ways to share measurements is a strong idea — but the UX guiding customers through standard size, self-measurement, and Clone My Jeans is unclear and causes drop-offs.

§03 · CHECKOUT

Checkout isn't built to convert

No gift messaging, no upsell opportunities at cart, and no abandoned checkout recovery properly configured. Every uncompleted purchase is a permanent loss.

§04 · AUTOMATION

Post-purchase flows are manual

No automated order confirmation with fit stylist call details, no alteration request flow triggered post-delivery, no reorder prompts for repeat buyers. All handled manually.

§05 · PRODUCT PAGES

PDPs don't sell the customisation

The core eniM proposition — 100% custom made, any size, free alterations — is buried. Product pages need to surface the differentiation that justifies the premium.

§06 · MOBILE

Mobile experience needs work

The majority of eniM's traffic is mobile. The sizing flow, product customisation options, and checkout are not optimised for a one-handed, small-screen experience.
