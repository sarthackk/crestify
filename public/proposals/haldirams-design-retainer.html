01The Opportunity
The store is the campaign.

Haldiram's stores run on more than product — every counter, wall and table communicates the brand. Menu cards, tent cards, in-store posters and seasonal promotional pieces are touchpoints customers interact with directly, and they deserve the same design discipline as any digital campaign.

This proposal covers a dedicated design retainer for exactly that: the offline, printable collateral used across Haldiram's stores — distinct from social media or digital marketing assets, which are not part of this scope.
