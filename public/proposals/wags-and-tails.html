00Overview
Built around the pet profile.

A mobile platform that lets pet parents book grooming at home and dog walks on demand — and lets your groomers and walkers pick up and complete those jobs from their own app. Everything hangs off the pet: breed, age, weight, vaccination status, allergies, temperament and care notes. Bookings are made for a pet, history is kept against a pet, and the owner's care notes are surfaced directly to whoever turns up. That single thread is the difference between a booking tool and a service your customers stay with.

We release in three working milestones, walking first. Dog walking is a new service line that genuinely needs an app to exist — an on-demand, GPS-tracked walk isn't something you can run over WhatsApp — so it goes live first and starts earning while the grooming module is still in build. Grooming already works today; the app makes it better rather than making it possible.

Working prototype
A clickable prototype of all three roles is live at wags-and-tails.vercel.app — use the role switcher, top right, to move between the customer, groomer and walker apps. This is design intent, not the finished product; flows, wording and visuals are open to your input before development starts.

What we're building.

Two native apps and two web portals — delivered as eight connected modules on a single stack.

01

Customer App

Phone-number sign-up, multiple pet profiles per account, a service catalogue with package inclusions, grooming booking (package, add-ons, slot), on-demand and scheduled walk booking, live walk tracking on a map, in-app payments, booking history with one-tap rebooking, and ratings for partners.

02

Partner App — Walker Priority

Partner sign-up and profile, an online/offline availability toggle, incoming walk requests with a countdown to accept or decline, pet details and owner care notes on arrival, GPS route tracking with live duration, distance and pace, photo capture during the walk, an issue-report, end-of-walk summary, and an earnings dashboard.

AvailabilityGPS RouteLive SessionEarnings
03

Partner App — Groomer Phase 2

Availability and service area, an open job feed with payout and distance, job claiming with auto-assignment to schedule, job detail with pet profile and care notes, navigation to the address, a checklist generated from the booked package with add-ons tracked separately, before/after photo capture, completion gated on a finished checklist, a day/week schedule, and earnings.

Job FeedPackage ChecklistBefore/AfterSchedule

The Pet Profile

The spine of the product. Every pet a customer owns — breed, age, weight, sex, photo, vaccination records and due dates, allergies, coat type, temperament, vet contact and microchip details. Care notes written by the owner are carried through to the groomer or walker who turns up, so the service always knows the animal.
