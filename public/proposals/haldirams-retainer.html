01The Opportunity
Strong fundamentals. No standing owner.

Haldiram's UK runs a well-built Shopify store — clear category architecture, a tiered offer structure, a strong review base, and a heritage brand few UK competitors can match.

What it lacks is standing technical ownership. We reviewed the live storefront on 22 July and found 19 specific, fixable issues quietly costing conversions. None are large. All are the kind only caught when someone looks at the store every week — which is exactly what this retainer provides.
