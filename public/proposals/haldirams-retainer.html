01The Opportunity
Strong fundamentals. No standing owner.

Haldiram's UK runs a well-built Shopify store with genuinely strong fundamentals: a clear category architecture, a tiered offer structure, an established review base, and a heritage brand that most competitors in the UK Indian-food category cannot match.

What the store lacks is consistent, hands-on technical ownership. Ahead of preparing this proposal we reviewed the live storefront — the homepage and product pages — and found a series of specific, fixable issues that are quietly costing conversions every day. None of them are large problems. All of them are the kind of thing that only gets caught and fixed when someone is looking at the store every week.

That is what this retainer provides: a dedicated Shopify team on standing weekly availability, covering development, store management, and continuous conversion improvement — starting with the findings set out below.

2.6Requires store access to assess

The following cannot be evaluated from the public storefront and will be addressed in the first two weeks of the engagement:

  • Funnel data — Shopify Analytics will show whether the largest drop-off is homepage → collection, product → cart, or cart → checkout, allowing the priorities above to be re-ranked against real data.
  • Mobile experience — sticky add-to-cart, drawer behaviour, tap-target sizing, and navigation tested on real devices.
  • Checkout configuration — express payment wallets, shipping-rate display at checkout, and abandoned-checkout recovery.
