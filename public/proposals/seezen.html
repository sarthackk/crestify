00Overview
One system to run the café.

Seezen gets a connected café platform — the parts that guests touch and the parts that run the back of house, built to work together. Guests scan a QR code and browse a live menu; your team manages stock and recipes in an inventory app; and a loyalty program quietly turns first-time visitors into regulars.

We roll it out in phases so value lands fast: the QR menu goes live within 5 days, and the inventory and loyalty apps follow over the next few weeks — no waiting months to see anything working.
