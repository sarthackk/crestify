00Overview
A store as crafted as the jewellery.

Jouell sells lightweight everyday luxury — Kundan & Polki, pearl, American diamond and oxidised pieces that carry real heritage detail. The store should carry that same detail. This is a full custom revamp: we rebuild the storefront from scratch, designed and coded to Jouell's jewel-tone identity, not adapted from a marketplace theme.

Design, development, custom features, catalogue and every integration are delivered as one build, with a single handover. The result is a faster, richer, conversion-focused store — collections that browse by style and material, product pages that do the jewellery justice, and a checkout built to convert.
