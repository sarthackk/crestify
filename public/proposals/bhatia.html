01 The Brief
What we're building.

Bhatia Hospitality runs multiple brands and outlets — Sidewalk, Common Time, and more. Today, operations are spread across WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, and word of mouth, with no central control over who sees what or where things are.

This project delivers a single internal super-app: a secure place for teams to communicate, a clear access structure so each role sees only what it should, live tracking of every physical asset by barcode, and inventory that managers can act on. Mobile-first for floor staff and managers, with a web console for group administration.

02 In Scope
The four modules.

Every piece of the group's internal operations, designed and built as one cohesive product.

01

Secure Chat

Internal messaging scoped by outlet and role. Anti-leak layer: screenshot protection, identity watermarking on every screen, in-app-only file viewing, and a download-on-request flow approved by the super admin.

Role-scoped channels Watermarking Screenshot protection Download approval
02

Access Management

Group → brand → outlet → user hierarchy. Four roles — Super Admin, Admin, Outlet Manager, Staff — each with a granular permission set. A web console to manage outlets, people, and approvals.

4-role hierarchy Web console Granular permissions Approval flows
03

Asset Tracking

A QR/barcode for every table, chair, and machine. Scan to view history, scan to report broken or missing with a photo. A live per-outlet registry with a full audit trail.

QR/Barcode gen Scan to report Photo upload Audit trail
04

Inventory

Per-outlet stock with par levels and low-stock alerts, stock in/out and wastage logging, and inter-outlet transfer requests routed through chat for approval.

Par levels Low-stock alerts Wastage log Transfer requests
iOS + Android app Web super-admin console Clickable prototype (early) Full audit logging
