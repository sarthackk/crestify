03Hours & dedication
One developer, every day.

3–4 focused hours a day, every working day — roughly 70 hours a month, all on Galli. It’s a real, consistent block of development time, not on-call scraps. If a month runs bigger than the daily block allows, we flag it early and either spread the work across weeks or agree extra hours before doing them.

Good to know
The retainer is development time, not a fixed deliverable list — that’s what lets a drop page one week and a batch of fixes the next both fit. Larger, self-contained builds (a full new template, a big custom feature) can run as a separate fixed-price project alongside the retainer.
