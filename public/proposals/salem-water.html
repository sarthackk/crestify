00Introduction & Understanding
Authentic information, one source of truth.

Crestify is a full-stack product development studio in Kanpur. Our in-house team of designers and developers builds custom web platforms, dashboards and admin systems end to end — from requirement gathering through deployment and support. This proposal responds to SCWSPL’s scope document SLM-MIS-01-P-0 and follows its sitemap, milestones and compliance requirements.

SCMC needs a single, trusted platform for authentic, current information — in English and Tamil — that non-technical staff can manage, that complies with GIGW and WCAG 2.1 AA, and that deploys on NIC, a MeitY-empanelled cloud or other SCMC-approved infrastructure. Consumer services (bill payment, new connections, grievance redressal) stay on their existing external portals and are linked from this website.
